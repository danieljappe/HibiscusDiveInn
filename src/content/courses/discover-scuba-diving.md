A briefing in shallow water, then a supervised dive on the house reef. You do
not come away certified — this is about finding out whether you want to carry
on.

If you decide you do, ask us whether what you cover here counts towards the
Open Water course — it depends on the agency, and it is worth knowing before
you book either.
