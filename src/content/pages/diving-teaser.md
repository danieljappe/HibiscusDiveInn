Bantayan sits on the edge of the Visayan Sea, and the diving here is calm,
shallow and unhurried. There is one instructor, which means small groups and
no queue for the boat.

Courses run from first-time try-dives through to certification. Certified
divers can be in the water the same day they arrive.
