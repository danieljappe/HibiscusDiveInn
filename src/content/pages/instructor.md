This is a one-instructor operation. The person who answers your message is the
person who briefs you, gets in the water with you, and signs off your
certification.

That is the part worth knowing before you book. In a larger centre you are
assigned whoever is free that morning. Here you are not — you dive with the
same person all week, and by the second day they know how you use your air and
what you are nervous about.
