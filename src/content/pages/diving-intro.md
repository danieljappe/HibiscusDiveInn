Bantayan sits at the north-west corner of Cebu, away from the busier dive
destinations further south. The water is warm, the reef starts at the end of
the jetty, and most days the conditions are easy.

Because there is one instructor, we take a handful of divers at a time. You
will not be following a group of twenty around a reef, and you will not be
waiting for a boat to fill up.
