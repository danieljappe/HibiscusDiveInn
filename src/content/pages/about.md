Hibiscus Dive Inn is a small inn on Bantayan Island, off the north-west coast of
Cebu. There are a handful of rooms, a house reef, and one dive instructor. That
is the whole operation.

Guests tend to be divers, or people who are about to become divers. Days here
are quiet and unstructured: dive in the morning, eat, sleep through the heat of
the afternoon, walk to the water again before dark.

We answer enquiries ourselves, usually within a day. If you want to know
whether something is possible, ask — the answer is often yes, and if it is no,
we will say so.
